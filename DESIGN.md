## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-stakes B2B heavy-industry engineering, operational rigor, and strategic capital allocation. Designed for agricultural industrialists, plant directors, food processing conglomerates, and turnkey dairy investors, it balances technological authority with tangible machinery reliability.

### Design Movement
**Corporate / Modern Industrial**: Combines clinical stainless-steel precision with structured editorial layouts. It relies on architectural line rules, high-contrast metric callouts, rich petroleum teals, and engineering-grade amber/ochre highlights. Surfaces prioritize dense legibility, deliberate structural framing, and clear process flow diagrams over decorative fluff.

### Emotional Response
- **Decisive Authority**: Communicates technical mastery and unbiased consulting ("Consultancy first. Everything else follows.").
- **Engineering Precision**: Visual structures feel calibrated, hygienic, and solid like modern dairy processing lines.
- **Strategic Confidence**: Converts raw market data and complex plant blueprints into intelligible business imperatives.

## Colors

The color palette reflects hygienic industrial engineering: deep petrol/petroleum teal provides an anchor of trust and authority, counterbalanced by warm ochre gold representing capital efficiency, active milestones, and diagnostic attention.

### Color Tokens & Usage
- **Primary (`#0A3642` & `#0B3C49`)**: Foundational canvas color for hero panels, dark engineering overviews, key structural banners, and dominant display text. Conveys stability, operational integrity, and strategic seriousness.
- **Secondary (`#D69E2E` & `#C98A2C`)**: Used strictly for selective emphasis, highlighted keywords within headlines, stage node milestones, process badges, and primary calls to action.
- **Tertiary (`#0E4D5D`)**: Intermediate mid-teal for secondary cards, table header strips, interactive hover states, and structural dividers.
- **Neutral Base (`#F4F6F7` to `#FFFFFF`)**: Clinical, pristine background washes. Mimics sterile, pharmaceutical-grade cleanroom environments and ensures maximum contrast for data visualization.
- **Neutral Surface Dark (`#06232B`)**: Deepest contrast tone for footers, code-level process schemas, and system status drawers.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Space Grotesk** for display headlines, industrial metrics, and badges with **Work Sans** for high-clarity technical body text.

### Architectural Rules
- **Display Headlines**: Use uppercase selectively on short, high-impact statements (`SEVEN CAPABILITIES. ONE CATEGORY.`). Maintain tight tracking on large metrics and titles.
- **Accented Emphasis**: Alternate between Primary Petrol Teal (`#0A3642`) and Warm Ochre (`#D69E2E`) inside compound titles (e.g., "The **8 STAGES** of a Greenfield Dairy Project").
- **Quantitative Metrics**: The `stat-metric` token is reserved for performance statistics, capacity throughputs, and financial or biological counts (e.g., `97%`, `74.7M`, `30,000,000/ml`). It must always be paired with a concise `body-sm` or `label-md` description.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a rigid 12-column engineering grid on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column format on tablets and a 4-column system on mobile devices.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px)**: 12-column layout, 48px canvas margin, 24px gutters. Multistage timelines, facility galleries, and data columns run parallel to showcase full end-to-end capabilities without vertical compression.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column layout, 32px canvas margin, 20px gutters. 3-up and 4-up capability cards wrap to 2-column paired grids.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px)**: 4-column layout, 20px canvas margin, 16px gutters. Process stages transition into a vertical stepped spine with aligned node indicators.

### Density Philosophy
Components use compact, deliberate internal padding to preserve technical information density. Vertical whitespace between related items remains tight (`space-sm` to `space-md`), while section transitions rely on `space-2xl` to distinctly bracket strategic consultancy phases.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy, playful drop shadows in favor of crisp structural definition, surface-tinted layers, and subtle hairline dividers reminiscent of engineering blueprints.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level (`level-0`)**: Pure flat surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F4F6F7`) delineated with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(11, 60, 73, 0.12)`).
- **Interactive Card Level (`level-1`)**: Subtle engineered depth for clickable capability panels. Features a hard 1px border paired with an ambient tinted shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(10, 54, 66, 0.08)`.
- **Raised Module / Modal (`level-2`)**: For diagnostic inspectors, interactive process calculators, and mobile drawer navigations: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 54, 66, 0.16)`.
- **Tonal Contrast Layering**: Major focal shifts are achieved by alternating light grey/slate background zones (`#F4F6F7`) with full-bleed petroleum canvas blocks (`#0A3642`), creating clear thematic separation without elevation drift.

## Shapes

The shape system employs a crisp, soft profile (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are mildly softened to prevent visual harshness while maintaining the mechanical, disciplined precision appropriate for industrial manufacturing.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Input fields, buttons, table cell highlights, and data chips.
- **Structural Containers (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Capability photo tiles, milestone cards, stage nodes, and modal containers.
- **Circular Badges (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for numbered timeline checkpoints, equipment schematic icons, and circular phase indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Ochre Gold (`#D69E2E`) background with dark petrol text (`#0A3642`), uppercase `label-lg` typography, `0.25rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Hover shifts to `#C98A2C` with subtle forward arrow displacement.
- **Secondary / Technical Action**: Transparent background with a `1.5px solid #0A3642` border, petrol text, and an active hover state that fills to `#0A3642` with crisp white text.
- **Dark Mode / Inverted Action**: Used on petrol backdrops; solid white or amber fill with petrol text.

### Capability & Phase Cards
- **Structure**: 2-part modular layout consisting of an upper 4:3 industrial machinery photography block (clipped to `0.5rem` top radius) and a high-contrast label band across the bottom.
- **Label Banner**: Deep amber (`#D69E2E`) or petrol teal (`#0A3642`) block with bold, centered `label-lg` copy.
- **Data Cards**: Clean slate cards featuring hairline dividers separating numerical throughputs from narrative parameters.

### Process Milestones & Timelines
- **Vertical Pipeline**: A continuous `2px` vertical spine colored in `#D69E2E` or `#0E4D5D`.
- **Milestone Nodes**: Circular indicators (`36px × 36px`) centered on the spine, bearing step numbers in `Space Grotesk` bold. Alternating left/right horizontal connector rules route into descriptive phase text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Engineering Badges**: Uppercase micro-labels in `Space Grotesk` with `0.25rem` radius, subtle background tint (`rgba(214, 158, 46, 0.15)`), and solid `#C98A2C` border.
- **Status Tags**: Clear, high-contrast operational status markers (e.g., "Feasibility", "Commissioned", "R&D Phase").

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Base**: Pristine white background, `1px solid rgba(10, 54, 66, 0.2)` border, `0.25rem` corner radius, `Work Sans` text.
- **Focus State**: Replaces standard blue rings with a high-visibility `2px solid #0B3C49` ring and amber accent caret.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Selection Controls**: Squared with soft `2px` corners for checkboxes; true circular for radios. Selected state uses solid `#0A3642` fill with white indicator marks.
